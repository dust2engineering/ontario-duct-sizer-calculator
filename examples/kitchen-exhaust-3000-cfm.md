# Example 2 — Kitchen exhaust main, 3,000 CFM at 1,800 FPM

Free area = 3,000 / 1,800 = 1.67 ft2 = 240.0 in2.
Round: 17.5 in diameter -> use an 18 in round duct.
Rectangular options (equal area): 20 in x 12.0 in; 24 in x 10.0 in; 16 in x 15.0 in.
Grease ducts are run fast on purpose: the velocity keeps grease entrained to the fan instead of
settling in the duct.
