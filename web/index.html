Duct Sizer Calculator

Airflow (CFM) + velocity (FPM) → round and rectangular duct sizes. The everyday HVAC sizing step, in the open.

Your duct sizes will appear here.

The math is simple: duct free area = airflow / velocity. This tool turns a CFM and a target velocity into the round diameter and a family of rectangular sizes with the same free area, and shows the Huebscher equivalent diameter used to compare friction loss. Final duct design also checks friction rate, fittings, noise and the fan curve — this is the sizing starting point.
